<strong>Deserved Win %</strong>

With [Bill Connelly](https://bsky.app/profile/ESPNBillC.bsky.social)'s [Five Factors of Football](https://www.sbnation.com/college-football/2017/10/13/16457830/college-football-advanced-stats-analytics-rankings) in mind and inspired by soccer app [futi](https://futi.live), we built a custom post-game win expectancy model we call "Deserved Win %". 

<a href="https://gameonpaper.com/game/400869070"><img class="img img-responsive" width="100%" src="/assets/img/game-pgwe.png" title="2016 GT @ uga PGWE breakdown" alt="2016 GT @ uga PGWE breakdown" /></a>

We operationalized the Five Factors differently (more on that in a bit), but the premise remains the same: "based on the box score, can we say who _should_ have won this game?" We also generate an "impact" rating for each of the features that go into our model, so readers can generate quick insights about the game flow. For the above example, this allows us to say "georgia's relative inability to generate explosive plays and stay on schedule contributed heavily to them losing a close game". There's also a quarter-by-quarter breakdown, so you can draw these conclusions on a quarter by quarter basis as well throughout the game.

We've introduced a number of new sections on team-season pages:

<strong>Five Factors</strong>

<a href="https://gameonpaper.com/year/2026/team/228"><img class="img img-responsive" width="100%" src="/assets/img/team-season-five-factors.png" title="2026 Clemson - Five Factors" alt="2026 Clemson Five Factors" /></a>

Each team-season page now has a breakdown of the team's performance in each of [Bill Connelly](https://bsky.app/profile/ESPNBillC.bsky.social)'s [Five Factors of Football](https://www.sbnation.com/college-football/2017/10/13/16457830/college-football-advanced-stats-analytics-rankings). We've picked a few different metrics to represent each Factor:

- Efficiency: (EPA-based) success rate
- Explosiveness: (EPA-based) explosive play rate
- Field Position: 
    - average drive starting yardline
    - expected points for the average drive starting yardline
- Finishing Drives: points per scoring opportunity
- Turnovers:
    - Expected turnovers per game
    - Turnover luck (in points)
    - Havoc Rate

Definitions for all of these metrics can be found in our [glossary](/glossary).
