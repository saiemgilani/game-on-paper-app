Every processed game now carries a StatBroadcast-style **drive summary** —
per-team drive lines with two named drive-success metrics, points off
turnovers, forced three-and-outs, and time of possession by quarter, plus the
OBTAINED/HOW-LOST drive chart, how-scores-happened, and long-play lists — and
a **situational stats** block: down-by-down tendencies with distance buckets
and conversion attribution, red zone, finishing drives, rushing quality
tiers, passing profile, the 4th-down decision report, score-state splits,
situational penalties, havoc, turnovers, field zones, pace, and big plays.

All of it **windows with the quarter and half spans**, and every response now
includes the per-window slices for the advanced box, drive summary, and
situational stats — so span switching happens in place, no reload.

The computation lives in
[sportsdataverse-py](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-py)
(`create_drive_summary` / `create_situational_stats`, graduated from this
app), so every consumer of the library gets the same metrics.
