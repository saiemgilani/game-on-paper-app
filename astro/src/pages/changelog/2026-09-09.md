The site now has an NFL section at [/nfl](/nfl).

### New

- **NFL scoreboard, week schedules and game pages** at `/nfl`, `/nfl/year/<season>/type/<type>/week/<week>` and `/nfl/game/<id>`. Game pages carry everything the college pages do: the advanced box score, drives, every play with EPA and win probability, the win probability and expected points charts, situational splits and penalties.
- A **CFB ▸ NFL switch** in the header. Every page knows which league it is rendering, so links stay inside the league you are browsing.
- NFL games are processed by the same engine as college games, through `sportsdataverse-py`'s `NFLPlayProcess` and its NFL expected points / win probability models.

### Not yet

- NFL season leaderboards, team pages, the chart builder and percentile trends. Those need the NFL season tables on sportsdataverse.org, which are being built next; the header hides them for the NFL until they work.
